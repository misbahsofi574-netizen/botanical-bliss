Watering Your Plants

Watering is one of the most important parts of plant care. Avoid following a fixed schedule blindly. Check the soil first and water when the plant actually needs it.

  • Check the top layer of soil before watering.
  • Water thoroughly rather than giving only a few drops.
  • Make sure excess water can drain properly.
  • Avoid keeping plant roots constantly waterlogged.

Understanding Sunlight

Different plants need different amounts of light. Observe your plant and choose a location according to its light requirements.

  • Place sun-loving plants in bright locations.
  • Use bright indirect light for plants that prefer it.
  • Rotate indoor plants occasionally for even growth.
  • Watch for signs of too much or too little light.

Soil & Fertilizer

Healthy soil provides plants with support, water and nutrients. Choose soil according to the type of plant you are growing.

  • Use well-draining soil for most container plants.
  • Choose a suitable potting mix for indoor plants.
  • Use fertilizers according to the plant's needs.
  • Avoid over-fertilizing your plants.

Indoor Plant Care

Indoor plants need the right balance of light, water, humidity and airflow to remain healthy.

  • Keep plants in suitable lighting conditions.
  • Do not overwater plants.
  • Clean dusty leaves gently when needed.
  • Give plants enough space and airflow.

Common Plant Problems

Changes in leaves or growth can indicate that a plant needs attention. Look for symptoms and check the basic growing conditions first.

  • Yellow leaves may have several possible causes.
  • Check watering and drainage when growth looks unhealthy.
  • Inspect leaves regularly for visible pests.
  • Remove damaged leaves when appropriate.

Seasonal Gardening

Plant care can change with temperature, rainfall and sunlight. Adjust watering, placement and maintenance according to the season.

  • Monitor soil moisture more carefully during rainy periods.
  • Protect sensitive plants from extreme temperatures.
  • Adjust watering when weather conditions change.
  • Use seasonal conditions to plan new planting.

Gardening Note: Every plant is different. Always consider the specific needs of your plant, local climate and growing conditions.
